# Verra-Voile — Seed Round Business Plan

> AI-Powered, One-Stop Overseas Destination Wedding Platform for Chinese Couples



## 1. Problem

Approximately 7 million couples get married in China each year, with a growing percentage choosing overseas destination weddings. However, couples face the following core challenges during planning:

1. **Extremely Fragmented Discovery Channels** — No unified aggregation platform. Couples can only browse scattered guides on social platforms like Xiaohongshu, and must search for venues, photographers, and florists separately — inefficient and unreliable.
2. **Opaque Pricing** — No standardized venue pricing, vague package details, making it difficult for couples to estimate real costs and feel confident in their decisions.
3. **Inconsistent Information Quality** — Online content is hard to verify, with outdated information and paid promotions rampant, leaving couples without trustworthy references.
4. **No One-Stop Service** — Venues, photographers, florists, and dresses are scattered across different channels, with no platform connecting the entire planning workflow.



## 2. Solution

Verra-Voile is an **AI-powered, one-stop European destination wedding service platform** for Chinese couples, offering:

- **European Venue Database** — 500+ popular European wedding venues (castles, manors, churches, wineries), unified Chinese display with transparent pricing.
- **European Photographer Network** — Curated wedding photographers across Europe, with online portfolio browsing.
- **Flowers & Dress Shopping** — Connected to European suppliers for online selection and price comparison.
- **AI Smart Assistant** — Intelligent recommendations based on budget and style preferences; upload wedding videos or photos, and AI automatically captures scene details to generate matching venue, dress, and floral proposals.

**One-Liner:** Making European wedding planning as easy as shopping on Taobao for Chinese couples.
